Privacy Policy

Cyber Street Combat (Android app com.mostafa.motorush) • Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Cyber Street Combat ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used. The game is developed by an independent developer.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. Your progress (coins, gems, bikes, settings and scores) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, clicks, rewarded ads watched) and diagnostic data

This data is used for showing ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention and, where you allow it, personalised ads. It is handled under Google's policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change that choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Device permissions

The game uses the internet connection (to load ads) and, if you choose tilt steering, your device's motion sensor. Motion data is used only on the device to steer and is never stored or sent anywhere.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Security and retention

We do not store any personal data. Data processed by Google AdMob is transmitted encrypted and retained according to Google's policies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
